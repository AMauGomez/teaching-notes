Teaching

Teaching assistant at the Faculty of Sciences, UNAM (Aug 2023 – present): 13 course sections across 6 subjects of the statistics track

Author

Aaron Mauricio Gómez Jiménez

I lead the twice-weekly lab and problem sessions that accompany the professor’s lectures: I work through the theory with students, write the R code for each topic, and build case studies on real data so the methods are learned on questions that matter. Since 2023 I have assisted in 13 course sections with about 360 enrolled students.

How I teach

  • One real question per course. Inference is taught around a single claim tested end to end (is someone hit by a car every 30 minutes?); regression around whether mortgage brokers charge more.
  • Check the assumptions before the conclusion. Every method comes with its diagnostics and with an example where ignoring them gives the wrong answer.
  • Code anyone can rerun. All material is in R, organised by the official syllabus, in the teaching-notes repository.
